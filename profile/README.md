## Who we are

**AMANISES** is a small game modding team, named after the evernight goddess.
We make mods we want to play ourselves, and we build them in the open with our community of testers.

## Our mods

### BBSC - Bloodborne Seamless Co-op
One co-op session from the first lamp to the last boss: across areas, deaths, lamps, bosses,
the Hunter's Dream and Chalice Dungeons. Each hunter keeps their own levels, echoes and items.

- Download and install guide: [Seamless Hunt](https://install.seamlesshunt.com)
- Community server account: [seamlesshunt.com](https://seamlesshunt.com)
- Works with shadPS4 and the Bloodborne PC port
- Made together with Yharnam Together and Supermedo for the native port
